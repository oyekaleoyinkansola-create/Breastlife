Breast Health Guide

A private, simple guide to noticing changes, learning what they may mean, and knowing when to seek medical care.

Important: This app cannot test for or diagnose breast cancer. There is currently no reliable at-home app or HIV-strip-like self-test that can confirm or rule out breast cancer. Only a qualified healthcare professional can assess symptoms and arrange tests when needed.

1. Check for changes

Think about whether you have noticed any of these changes. This checklist is for awareness, not diagnosis.

2. Know what to do

01

Notice what is normal for you

Become familiar with how your breasts normally look and feel. There is no single “correct” way for breasts to look.

02

Do not wait for pain

Breast cancer may not cause pain early on. A new or persistent change deserves medical attention, even if it does not hurt.

03

Arrange a clinical assessment

Contact a healthcare professional or breast clinic promptly for a new or concerning change. They can decide whether an examination, imaging, or biopsy is appropriate.

04

Ask about screening

Screening recommendations depend on your age, personal and family history, and local guidance. Ask a qualified clinician what is suitable for you.

3. Plan a check-in

Use this optional reminder to plan when you will review breast-health information or contact a clinic. It is not a substitute for recommended screening.

Reminder details stay in this browser on this device. They are not sent to a clinic.
